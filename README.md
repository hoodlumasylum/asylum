# Hoodlum Asylum

Community site preview: creativity, support and showing up for each other.

**Live:** https://hoodlumasylum.com/

Front-end only (static HTML/CSS/JS). Posts you write stay in your browser. There is no login, no messaging backend, and no data is collected.

## Pages
- Home landing (category tiles and podcast carousel)
- Community feed, Explore, Create
- Mental Health Support (UK helplines)
- Podcast Hub, Artists & Creators
- Further section pages from the sidebar

## Honesty notes
- Sample posts, creators and podcasts are labelled as examples, with initial avatars, not photos of real people.
- Mental health: Samaritans 116 123 (free, 24/7), text SHOUT to 85258, NHS 111 (option 2), 999 in an emergency. Community chat, peer groups and sessions are coming soon. This is not clinical care.
- Joining, messages and notifications are coming soon.
