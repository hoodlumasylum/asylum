# Hoodlum Asylum

A community platform built on **Maat**: truth, balance, justice and harmony.

**Live:** https://hoodlumasylum.com/ (also https://hoodlumasylum.github.io/asylum/)

Front-end preview (static HTML/CSS/JS, hash routing, demo data in `localStorage`).

## Inside
- **Temple** home with the Maat story, the scales and the feather
- **Maat** page: the Weighing of the Heart, ib / ba / ka, and some of the earliest writings on the inner self
- **Halls** for music, mental health, creators, gaming, podcasts, film, spirit, fitness, style, community and gatherings
- **VS Arena**: talent battles (talent, not beef) with votes, chat and demo gifts
- **Concept Lab**: AI hosts, clearly badged as AI; concepts owned by Hoodlum Asylum Concept Lab
- **C-Notes**: demo balance only. No real payments
- **Wellbeing**: UK helplines (Samaritans 116 123, Shout 85258, NHS 111, 999 in an emergency). Peer support, not clinical advice

## Art
All artwork is original SVG/CSS made for this site. Fonts: Cinzel and Inter (Google Fonts).
